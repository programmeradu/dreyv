
## dreyv — Elegant Landing Page for Rideshare Drivers

A single-section, premium landing page that introduces dreyv to professional rideshare drivers.

### Page structure
- **Top nav**: dreyv wordmark on the left, a single subtle "Sign in" link on the right.
- **Hero (full viewport)**:
  - Refined headline (e.g., "Drive smarter. Earn more.")
  - Supporting subhead positioning dreyv as the premium companion app for rideshare drivers.
  - Primary CTA button ("Get early access") and secondary text link ("Learn more").
  - Elegant background — soft warm-neutral palette with a subtle gradient or grain, plus a tasteful visual accent (e.g., a faded steering-wheel/road silhouette or abstract luxury motif).
- **Footer**: Minimal — © dreyv + small tagline.

### Design direction
- **Mood**: Luxurious, refined, confident. Think premium automotive brand meets modern SaaS.
- **Palette**: Deep charcoal/near-black with warm ivory and a single muted gold/champagne accent. All tokens defined in the design system.
- **Typography**: Elegant serif display for the headline, clean sans-serif for body. Generous letter-spacing on the wordmark.
- **Details**: Generous whitespace, subtle fade-in on load, hover states with smooth transitions on the CTA.

### Out of scope (for now)
- No features grid, testimonials, or waitlist form yet — easy to add as next steps.
