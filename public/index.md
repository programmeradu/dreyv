# dreyv — AI treasury command layer for Solana & Web3 teams

> Non-custodial. Intent → Plan → Simulate → Sign. Solana-native.

dreyv replaces the 8+ disconnected tools treasury teams juggle (wallets, DEX aggregators, analytics, multisigs, chat) with a single AI workspace. Operators describe intent in plain language; dreyv compiles a plan, simulates it on a forked state, and proposes it to your multisig (e.g. Squads) for signing. Keys stay with you.

## What you can do
- Express treasury intent in plain language (CommandBar)
- Preview outcomes before signing (Foresight Simulator)
- Coordinate Squads multisig proposals with attached simulation
- Research onchain via Solana Atlas (tokens, MEV, rug detection, DCA, holders)
- Build mini-apps and agents in dreyv Studio
- Publish/install agents on the Marketplace (Anchor devnet)

## Why dreyv
- **Non-custodial** — keys stay in your wallet / Squads multisig
- **Simulation-first** — every plan is preview-able and explainable
- **Multisig-native** — Squads support out of the box
- **Solana-native** — Jupiter, Marinade, Jito, BlazeStake, Orca, Raydium, Helius
- **Agent-ready** — ACP, UCP, MCP, x402, OpenAPI, llms.txt, ai-plugin.json
